What these features mean
Charge proxy = K + R − D − E. This is a residue-count proxy, not pH-dependent net charge. Hydrophobic fraction uses AVILMFWY. GRAVY uses the Kyte–Doolittle scale. Molecular weight uses average amino-acid masses minus water for each peptide bond, assuming free termini and no modifications. Aromatic fraction uses FYW. Normalized composition entropy ranges from 0 to 1.
